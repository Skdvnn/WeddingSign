Printing them

Paper

  • 24 × 36 in, portrait — same poster as the welcome A-frame. Foam core, a frame at the entrance, or a second birch panel if you want the pair to match.
  • Walgreens: use JPEG for Walgreens under the chart you want. That file is a 24×36 poster at ~200 dpi (5400 × 8100 px) — above their 3600 × 5400 minimum. Photo.walgreens.com → Posters → upload → full resolution, not the compressed option. Pick 24 × 36, don’t crop or zoom. PNG and PDF won’t go through their photo lab cleanly (they convert PNG to JPEG; they reject PDF).
  • Bone stock if it’s paper next to the invites and 5×7s. Mustard only if this board is standing next to the painted welcome sign and you want them to read as a pair. Walgreens prints on photo paper (satin is the closest to bone); foam-core it after, or ask for a board print if they have 24×36.
  • Their cutter leaves about 0.2 in on each edge. Names already sit inside the sheet margin, so don’t add extra bleed.
  • Print dialog: the chosen paper size, no margins, scale 100%. That’s for a local printer or Save as PDF — not for the Walgreens upload.

The room

  • The poster is A–Z. Guests find their name, then the sign number. The room is still three longs and fifteen 5×7s — lay that out in Our seating.
  • One name per line, A–Z by last name — Hayley Adams under Adams, Patrick Smith under Smith. Allison and Skylar stay off the poster. The planner still seats a pair together.
  • Declined guests are ignored. First + last if two tables share a surname.
  • Figma: mock at 1728 × 2592 (24 × 36 in at 72 px/in). Use PNG for Figma under a chart, drop it in, export the frame at 2× for a poster.
